FAQ

“If you’re a top 10 student, why aren’t you in the honors program?”

Although I qualify for automatic enrollment in PRO mentorship, I haven’t joined PRO or Honors. I focus on global communities like the Microsoft Ambassador Program and AI OpenCV Program, personal projects, and quietly connecting with professionals. I also run free mentorship for first-generation students on resumes and career alignment, keeping my advice within the limits of my own experience. I’m also working toward CPA, CISA, Salesforce, and Microsoft QA certifications.

I’m interested in the space between a business problem and a working solution.

Since 2023, my work has spanned core business pipelines, technology infrastructure, enterprise automation, workflow improvement integrations, and personal AI projects. I enjoy understanding how systems work, finding what could work better, implementing focused frameworks that make a meaningful impact, and making the next step practical.

I want to share my perspective and work with organizations that make a positive impact on people. Whether that means improving everyday life or saving teams, managers, and partners hours of work, my number one goal is to improve the quality of life for others.
